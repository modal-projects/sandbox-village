    Under the hood

    While this panel is open, a running commentary appears bottom left: every sandbox starting and stopping, every command, every line spoken.

    Each villager's home is one Modal Sandbox. Asleep means the sandbox is gone and only a filesystem snapshot is kept; walking up, or a neighbour knocking, starts a new sandbox from it. While this panel is open, the houses are labelled with their sandbox or snapshot IDs.
